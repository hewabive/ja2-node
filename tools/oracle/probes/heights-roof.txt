# Shots that hit a soldier on a roof (README "Heights"), on SaveGame07: D13 in turn-based combat on our turn, the scene
# of heights.txt. Ira (2) crouches at 12867; enemy 23 crouches at 12397. Each block loads the save again, seeded, so it
# repeats.
#
# shotflight with animate= runs that soldier's animation beside the shooter's and goes on after the flight until the
# attack busy count is 0, so the target's hit animation plays to its end. A hit draws the impact sound's Random(3) in
# EVENT_SoldierGotHit on a roof as on the ground; 0033 marks it cosmetic.

# 1. A shot from the ground at enemy 23 on the east roof.
seed value=21 pre=reseed
save SaveGame07
shotplace who=23 grid=12885 level=1 life=100 lifeMax=100
shotflight who=2 target=12885 level=1 ap=80 animate=23

# 2. A shot from the east roof at enemy 23 on the north-east roof.
seed value=22 pre=reseed
save SaveGame07
shotplace who=2 grid=12885 level=1
shotplace who=23 grid=11296 level=1 life=100 lifeMax=100
shotflight who=2 target=11296 level=1 ap=80 animate=23

# 3. The same hit on the ground, enemy 23 moved two tiles west first: the load leaves the tiles around a soldier who has
# not moved since with no adjacency record (SetSoldierGridNo removes his records before it adds them, and the UINT8
# counts wrap back to 0), so a bullet passing beside him draws no suppression roll in the native build.
seed value=23 pre=reseed
save SaveGame07
shotplace who=23 grid=12395 level=0
shotflight who=2 target=12395 level=0 ap=80 animate=23
