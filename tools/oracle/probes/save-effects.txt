# The loaded sector's temp files after a native load (README "Savegame temp files"), on the saves
# save-effects-setup.txt writes into the oracle's home (run it first).

# 1. OracleSmoke: B13 with a tear gas cloud of radius 1 on Shadow's tile (6634). LoadSmokeEffectsFromMapTempFile
# spreads it before the soldiers enter the map; Sidney steps into it and Ira three tiles east of it, where the cloud
# reaches once it has aged: the second turn is 40 seconds later, four updates of DecaySmokeEffects. Shadow, saved
# without SOLDIER_GASSED, is gassed again as he enters the map (SetSoldierGridNo). The groups dump, before the clock
# moves, shows the sector flags: the map modifications flag cleared (no record written back), the smoke flag kept.
save OracleSmoke
groups
abclock ja2clock=6050930 last=6050930 rotting=0 base=1
abscreen screen=game
abcompress mode=1 on=0 paused=0
abset who=1 grid=6635
abset who=2 grid=6637
abturn
abclock seconds=164290
abturn
