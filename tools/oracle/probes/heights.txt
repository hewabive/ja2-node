# Heights of shots, throws and missiles (README "Heights"), on SaveGame07: D13 in turn-based combat on our turn. D13
# lies 80 px (409 height units) above the map's zero almost everywhere, so every shot and throw here runs on raised
# land. Ira (2) crouches at 12867; enemy 23 crouches at 12397. Buildings with flat roofs: the east one has its west wall
# on column 82 (12882) and roof tiles from 83 to 89 on rows 76-83 (12885); the north-east one roofs columns 93-100 on
# rows 66-75 (11296); a door stands at 12858. Each block loads the save again, seeded, so it repeats.
#
# shotplace puts a soldier on a tile and level (SetSoldierHeight, EVENT_SetSoldierPosition) and may set his life.
# shotheight prints SoldierToLocationChanceToGetThrough, AISoldierToLocationChanceToGetThrough and the point
# GetTargetWorldPositions aims at, for an empty tile; it draws nothing and restores the target fields.
# tossheight prints CalculateLaunchItemChanceToGetThrough (the landing tile and level, as the toss cursor or the AI asks)
# and the launch of CalculateLaunchItemParamsForThrow with no miss, for the hand item or item=; it draws nothing.
# shotflight runs HandleItem with the hand item at a tile and level, then frames of 100 ms: the shooter's animation
# frame, the game events, UpdateBullets and every physics object, until what it launched has stopped.
