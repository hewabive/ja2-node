Stub Pixel fonts of the ja2-node stub pack

stub-pixel-12.txt ("Stub Pixel 12") and stub-pixel-16b.txt ("Stub Pixel 16 Bold") are a subset of
Terminus Font 4.49.1 by Dimitar Toshkov Zhekov, converted to a plain text bitmap format. The fonts
built from them (fonts/stub-pixel-12.sti and fonts/stub-pixel-16b.sti in the stub pack) are
Modified Versions of the Font Software under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (OFL.txt).
As the license requires, they do not use the Reserved Font Name "Terminus Font".

          unchanged; regenerate with tools/stub-font-subset.ts.
