# Deliberate deviations from the original

Every place where ja2-node knowingly and permanently differs from JA2 Stracciatella with default settings (the
behaviour reference, `01` D1). An entry gives the original's behaviour in one clause with a `file:line` in
`reference/stracciatella`, what ja2-node does, and why. Behaviour that is not ported yet is not a deviation: it is an
entry of `gaps.md`. Other docs cite entries by their *Area* name (`deviations "Area"`); names are unique across both
files.

Most entries replace wall clocks, frames, sound lengths, message boxes, cursors and screens with game time, commands
and decision prompts, move cosmetic draws off the gameplay generator, keep saves free of side effects, or avoid the
original's undefined behaviour, hangs and softlocks.

## 2. Timing and clocks

Wall clocks, frames and sound lengths replaced by game time on 5 ms ticks (D3, `04` §3).

| Area | Original | ja2-node | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Timing | Wall-clock timers that never catch up; bullets, burst cadence and the real-time AI's 10 ms slice follow the frame rate and CPU (`Overhead.cc:1043`) | Fixed 5 ms ticks; the real-time AI visits every AI slot after its cursor each pass | Outcomes independent of FPS and CPU (`04` §3) |
| Speech | A line lasts its sample with speech on, else 60 ms per character, then 300 ms with the mouth closed (`Faces.cc:1206`, `:1304`) | Every line lasts the longer of its voice length (rule pack) and its text time, then 60 ticks; the viewer that finished first sends `skipLine {talkEnded}` and leaves only the 60 ticks | One simulation serves every viewer and must not depend on audio (`04` §3, `29` §4.5) |
| Skipping every line | No such setting | `speech: skip` (`newSession`, `setSpeech`) ends every line on the tick after it is said; a logged session setting, never saved | Agents playing with nobody listening (`29` §10) |
| Watchdogs | A 15 s AI deadlock breaker and a 25 s UI busy breaker (`AIMain.cc:366-368`, `Handle_UI.cc:405-418`) | Not ported | A hang is a bug and must fail tests |
| Frame timers | `TIMECOUNTERDONE` against the wall clock, polled every 5 ms (`Timer_Control.cc:15`) | Absolute game-ms deadlines checked on 5 ms ticks, same reset points | Same cadence without wall-clock jitter (`01` §3.3) |

| Enemy sighted on their turn | A 3 s wall-clock pause (`GameScreen.cc:315`) | 3000 game ms (`handleEnemySightingPause`); the simulation is not idle meanwhile | Game time (`10`) |
| Bullet flight | `MoveBullet` runs once per frame, gated by a 2–3 ms wall clock (`LOS.cc:3497-3504`) | One tile per 5 ms tick, the first inside `FireWeapon`; knives every 30 ms | Outcomes independent of FPS (`11`) |
| Game events | Events fire in the frame's event pump (`GameScreen.cc:456`) | Same queue order, drained after each tick's soldier pass | No frame loop (`11`) |
